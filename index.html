CalorieTracker Account Deletion

If you want to delete your CalorieTracker account and associated data, please send an email to: support.calorietracker@gmail.com

Use the subject: Delete CalorieTracker Account

Please include the email address used for your CalorieTracker account.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account email
  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Saved app data associated with your account, if stored on our services

Data that may be retained

Some technical logs may be retained for security, debugging, or legal reasons for a limited period.
