CalorieTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

CalorieTracker is a nutrition and calorie tracking app developed by CalorieTracker Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address for authentication, login, email verification, and password reset purposes.

The app may process food and nutrition information such as calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, saved foods, favorite foods, and meal entries.

If you use the Scan Food feature, the image you provide may be sent to our server or an AI service for analysis. The image is used to provide nutrition-related results.

If you use the Scan Barcode feature, barcode or product information may be processed to help identify nutrition information.

How we use information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To provide calorie and nutrition tracking features
  • To analyze food images and barcode information
  • To improve app functionality and reliability

Data sharing

We do not sell user data. Some data may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase for authentication and server or AI services for food image analysis.

Data security

Data is transmitted using secure connections where supported. We take reasonable steps to protect user information.

Account deletion

Users can request account deletion by visiting: https://stecsenoob.github.io/calorietracker-support/
